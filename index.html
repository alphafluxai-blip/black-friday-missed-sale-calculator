Quick questions

Are these my real losses?

No. Every figure here is an estimate calculated from the three numbers you enter, using the formula shown above. Your actual losses depend on how many missed callers would have bought, which only your own call records can tell you. Use this to size the problem, not as an audited figure.

I don’t know my missed-call percentage. What should I enter?

Start with your best guess. If your phone rings out during busy periods, at lunch, or after hours, try 20–30% and see the estimate. You can also check your phone system or provider’s call log for unanswered calls and divide by total calls.

Why Friday to Monday?

The Black Friday weekend runs 4 days: Black Friday (Fri 27 Nov), Saturday 28 Nov, Sunday 29 Nov and Cyber Monday (Mon 30 Nov 2026). Callers who cannot reach you on any of those days are just as likely to buy elsewhere.

What happens on the 15-minute demo?

You see the missed-call text-back system working live: a call is deliberately missed, the caller receives a text back within 60 seconds, the conversation continues by text, and the enquiry appears in the log. You can ask anything — there is no obligation to buy.

Does the system replace my team?

No. It only covers the calls nobody could answer. Your team still handles every conversation as normal — the text-back simply stops a missed caller from disappearing before your team gets a chance.
